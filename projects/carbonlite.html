PROJECT OVERVIEW

About CarbonLite

CarbonLite was developed as part of a Technopreneurship team project at Universiti Teknologi PETRONAS.

The concept focuses on the gap between environmental awareness and actual behaviour. Although many students understand the importance of sustainability, maintaining consistent eco-friendly habits can be difficult without motivation, feedback or measurable progress.

CarbonLite addresses this problem through a gamified mobile platform where students complete sustainability challenges, submit proof of their actions, earn points, track estimated carbon savings and redeem rewards.

The platform was also designed to provide universities with aggregated sustainability data that could support environmental initiatives and institutional reporting.

THE PROBLEM

Awareness does not always become action

University students may understand the importance of sustainability but still struggle to convert that awareness into consistent everyday behaviour.

Traditional environmental campaigns are often temporary, passive or difficult to measure. Students may participate in a one-time activity without receiving continuous motivation or visibility into their long-term environmental impact.

CarbonLite was proposed as a way to make sustainable behaviour more interactive, measurable and rewarding.

PROPOSED SOLUTION

Turning sustainable habits into a digital experience

Weekly Eco-Challenges

Students complete practical sustainability activities such as reducing plastic use, walking instead of driving and conserving electricity.

Action Verification

Activities can be verified using methods such as QR-code scanning, photo submission, timestamps or location-based evidence.

Carbon Impact Tracking

Completed activities are translated into estimated carbon savings so students can understand the impact of their actions.

Points & Rewards

Students earn points for completed sustainability activities and can redeem rewards such as vouchers or merchandise.

Leaderboards & Badges

Gamification elements encourage continued participation through achievement badges, rankings and friendly competition.

Analytics Dashboard

Users can monitor activity, carbon savings, points and progress, while universities can view aggregated sustainability insights.

STAKEHOLDERS

Creating value across the ecosystem

Universities

Student Organisations

BUSINESS STRATEGY

Proposed revenue model

CarbonLite explored several potential revenue streams to support the platform while maintaining accessibility for students.

  • University Licensing — institutions could subscribe for access to administrative dashboards, reporting and campaign management.
  • Student Premium Subscription — optional premium features could provide additional challenges, rewards and progress insights.
  • Sponsored Rewards — eco-conscious businesses could sponsor rewards in return for visibility and student engagement.

DATA-DRIVEN DESIGN

Analytics as part of the product

Individual Progress

Students could view their completed activities, points and estimated carbon savings.

Engagement Metrics

Participation and challenge-completion data could help identify engagement patterns.

Institutional Reporting

Aggregated data could help universities understand sustainability participation across faculties, clubs or campaigns.

Sustainability Impact

Carbon-equivalent estimates provide users with a measurable representation of their environmental contribution.
